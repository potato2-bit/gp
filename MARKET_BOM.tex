\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Every requirement frozen in the design package
(\fname{prototype\_design\_report.tex} and
\fname{output/tec\_robot\_results.json}) is matched here to one purchasable
part with a manufacturer name, part number, datasheet value, margin, vendor,
and unit price in Egyptian pounds. Two quantities are carried per line: the
4-tile bench demonstrator that the five-day plan builds now, and the full
69-tile robot priced for the thesis. Prices flagged \liveflag{} were read from
vendor web stores by script on 12 June 2026; prices flagged \indflag{} are
indicative and must be confirmed before purchase. The bench demonstrator
totals \num{28475}~EGP ($\approx$\num{593}~USD), against the
\num{5760}~EGP figure in the design report; Section~\ref{sec:rollup}
reconciles the gap line by line, most of it the imported encoder drivetrain
and the Raspberry~Pi that later design documents added. The full robot prices
at \num{218360}~EGP ($\approx$\num{4549}~USD), with the station battery bank
and the MPPT controllers as the two cost drivers. One material finding from
the live check: Pololu has renumbered its 37D gearmotor line, so the
shortlisted items \#4745/\#4741 no longer carry the specifications recorded in
the project transcript, and the selection moves to \#4756 and \#4743.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Sourcing rules}
Parts were selected in three steps. First, the governing requirement was taken
from the frozen design outputs: the verified results file
\fname{output/tec\_robot\_results.json}, the equations of
\fname{prototype\_design\_report.tex}, and the two-tier compute architecture
of \fname{ROS2\_ARCHITECTURE.tex}. Second, candidates were drawn from the
Egyptian market first: Future Electronics Egypt \cite{futelec2026store}, RAM
Electronics \cite{ramshop2026}, Cairo solar and marine distributors, the
computer-mall used market, and Amazon.eg as the local fallback. Imports
(Pololu direct) are accepted only where no local part meets the requirement,
and each import line carries a customs and lead-time note. Third, among
candidates that pass, the line goes to the part with the best
specification-to-requirement margin per pound, and every single-source line
gets a named fallback in Table~\ref{tab:risk}.

\subsection{Price provenance}
Future Electronics Egypt exposes machine-readable prices and stock flags, and
every figure taken from it on 12 June 2026 is marked \liveflag{} unmodified.
Pololu product pages also returned live catalogue prices the same day. RAM
Electronics runs an Odoo storefront that renders prices client-side, so
scripted reads return empty product blocks; RAM lines and all local-market
lines carry indicative prices marked \indflag{}, checked against recent
catalogue-typical figures on 12 June 2026 and to be confirmed at the counter.
Stock-depth numbers are not quoted anywhere in this document because no
vendor exposes them; in-stock flags are reported only where the store
returned one.

\subsection{Exchange rate and landed-cost factor}
All conversions use \num{48}~EGP/USD, the working rate of the Egypt-market
supplement \fname{tec\_deployment\_analysis\_v1.1}, which documents the
EGP~47--50/USD band holding through 2025--2026 on Central Bank of Egypt data.
Courier imports follow the same supplement's compound: roughly
\SI{15}{\percent} freight and insurance, then \SI{5}{\percent} duty and
\SI{14}{\percent} VAT on (CIF + duty), a landed-cost factor of about
\num{1.38} on the catalogue price. Landed EGP figures built this way are
marked \indflag{} even when the underlying catalogue price is live, because
the freight and brokerage share is an estimate.

\section{Subsystem Notes}
\label{sec:notes}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{Drive}
The frozen numbers are \SI{32.0}{\newton} of resistive force on the \ang{24}
slope (Eq.~7.1), \SI{1.37}{\newton\metre} of traction torque through a
\num{0.7}-efficient drivetrain, \SI{0.686}{\newton\metre} per motor, and
\SI{1.03}{\newton\metre} after the 1.5 safety factor; the \SI{0.05}{\metre\per\second}
working speed on \SI{0.03}{\metre} wheels means \SI{15.9}{\rpm} at the shaft
\cite{siegwart2011mobilerobots}. The project transcript shortlisted Pololu
\#4745 as a 50:1 encoder gearmotor, but the live product pages show Pololu
has since renumbered the 37D line: \#4745 is now a 100:1 unit with no encoder
\cite{pololu4745}, which fails the odometry requirement outright. The
selection therefore moves to \#4756, the 131:1 helical 37D with a 64~CPR
encoder \cite{pololu4756}. The gearbox choice falls out of the numbers. At
131:1 the no-load speed is \SI{76}{\rpm}, so cruise sits at \SI{21}{\percent}
of no load with the loaded speed at the margined torque still near
\SI{58}{\rpm}, and the \SI{25}{\percent}-of-stall continuous band
(\SI{11.3}{\kilogram\centi\metre}) covers the full \SI{1.03}{\newton\metre}
margined demand, not just the unmargined one. The 100:1 encoder unit
(\#4755) was rejected because its continuous band
(\SI{8.5}{\kilogram\centi\metre}) covers only the unmargined demand, and the
50:1 encoder unit (\#4753) because cruise would sit at \SI{8}{\percent} of no
load, where PWM resolution and encoder PI control are poorest. The encoder
gives about \num{8400} counts per wheel revolution, or \SI{0.02}{\milli\metre}
of travel per count: far finer than ROS~2 odometry needs. Local fallback if
customs stalls the parcel: four GA25-370 encoder gearmotors
(\num{445}~EGP\liveflag{}, in stock at Future Electronics), two per side,
which halves the per-motor demand and restores a 2.6$\times$ stall margin.

\subsection{Cleaning}
A cleaning event sprays \SI{3.6}{\liter} along the \SI{230}{\second}
boustrophedon path, a mean of \SI{0.94}{\liter\per\minute}. The Seaflo
21-series diaphragm pump delivers \SI{3.8}{\liter\per\minute} against a
\SI{2.4}{\bar} internal pressure switch \cite{seaflo2023pump}; the switch
dead-heads safely when dusty nozzles clog, which a plain centrifugal pump
does not. The \num{320}~EGP brushless pump listed at Future Electronics was
rejected: it matches the flow number but produces about \SI{0.3}{\bar},
too little for flat-fan spray nozzles. The bench demo does not need marine
hardware, so it carries an R385-class mini diaphragm pump instead. The brush
is a \SI{250}{\milli\metre} microfibre roller on the Pololu \#4743 (50:1,
\SI{200}{\rpm}, \SI{2.06}{\newton\metre} stall): 2.6$\times$ the
\SI{8}{\kilogram\centi\metre} roller estimate at the right surface speed. The
transcript's \#4741 now maps to a 19:1, \SI{5}{\kilogram\centi\metre} unit
\cite{pololu4741}, below the roller estimate, so it was dropped for the same
catalogue-drift reason as the drive motor.

\subsection{AWG thermal stack}
The tile is fixed by design: TEC1-12706, 69 units full scale, 4 on the bench
\cite{tec12706ds}. The costing basis of \num{130}~EGP survived the market
check with room to spare; Future Electronics lists the part in stock at
\num{120}~EGP \cite{futelec2026tec}. Each driven tile rejects up to
\SI{90}{\watt} on the hot side. On the bench, used CPU tower coolers
(\SI{95}{\watt} TDP class, about \num{300}~EGP in the computer mall) are the
cheapest hardware that meets this with a fan already mounted. At 69 tiles
that approach was rejected: it would cost over \num{20000}~EGP and weigh too
much, so the full robot uses nine banks of extruded aluminium fin profile
(\SI{13}{\centi\metre} of \qtyproduct{120 x 40}{\milli\metre} section per
tile) under forced draft from the 36 fans of the energy budget. Those 36 fans
at \SI{2.5}{\watt} over a 12-hour harvest are \SI{1080}{\watt\hour\per\dy},
which is the \SI{20.3}{\percent} fan share of the \SI{5.32}{\kilo\watt\hour}
daily load in the frozen energy table, so the fan line is sized exactly, not
estimated.

\subsection{Sensing}
The sensor set follows the supervisory state machine: DHT22 for the dew-point
decision \cite{dht22ds}, DS18B20 waterproof probes for the frost guard on the
cold plates \cite{ds18b20ds}, INA219 for battery and PV telemetry
\cite{ina219ds}, MPU-6050 for the tilt trip and odometry fusion
\cite{mpu6050ds}, and four lever microswitches for panel-edge detection. The
SHT31 was considered against the DHT22 and rejected on price and stock: the
DHT22 is live at \num{285}~EGP and in stock, meets the $\pm$\SI{0.5}{\celsius}
and $\pm$\SI{2}{\percent}~RH need, and the five-day plan already buys a spare
and cross-checks it against an ice-point-calibrated DS18B20. The INA219 is
the one judgement line: Future Electronics does not list it, RAM normally
stocks it, and the fallback is a shunt resistor on the ESP32 ADC, so the line
carries price risk but no schedule risk.

\subsection{Compute}
The two-tier architecture is frozen in \fname{ROS2\_ARCHITECTURE.tex}: a
Raspberry Pi 4B (\SI{4}{\giga\byte}) runs ROS~2 Jazzy for navigation,
coverage planning, and supervision \cite{rpi2021brief}, and an ESP32 runs
micro-ROS plus the hard-real-time loops over a \num{115200}-baud UART
\cite{esp32ds,belsare2023microros}. The plain 30-pin ESP32 DevKit is live at
\num{260}~EGP and in stock; the ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 (live, \num{550}~EGP) was
rejected because the architecture document's pin map and micro-ROS port
target the classic part and the S3 doubles the price for capability the
design never uses. For the Pi, the alternative is a Pi~5 at similar Egyptian
retail; the Pi~4 stays selected because the architecture document and its
serial-budget arithmetic were validated on it, and ROS~2 Jazzy runs on
either.

\subsection{Power}
Bench power is a mains question: four tiles at full drive draw about
\SI{24}{\ampere} at \SI{12}{\volt}, so the frozen \SI{10}{\ampere} floor is
necessary but not sufficient, and the Mean Well LRS-350-12
(\SI{29}{\ampere}) covers the worst case with \SI{20}{\percent} headroom
\cite{meanwell2023lrs}. A generic \num{400}~EGP 12V/10A brick was rejected:
it would cap the bench at one or two driven tiles. Full-scale supply follows
the frozen sizing: \SI{1147}{\Wp} of PV from two Jinko \SI{580}{\Wp} modules
(\SI{1.1}{\percent} over target) \cite{jinko2024tigerneo}, two EPever Tracer
6415AN controllers splitting the \SI{86}{\ampere} charge current
\cite{epever2023tracer}, and a \SI{698}{\ampere\hour} requirement met by four
\SI{200}{\ampere\hour} LiFePO$_4$ packs (\SI{800}{\ampere\hour},
\SI{15}{\percent} over). Two LONGi LR5-72HPH-545M modules were the
shortlisted alternative \cite{longi2022himo5}; at \SI{1090}{\Wp} they fall
\SI{5}{\percent} short of target, so the LONGi stays the named drop-in whose
datasheet dimensions drive the CAD envelope while the Jinko pair takes the
BOM line. The robot's own battery is a separate small line: a local
\SI{12}{\volt} \SI{20}{\ampere\hour} LFP pack with a \SI{20}{\ampere} BMS
covers the \SI{13}{\ampere} worst-case mobile draw at 1.5$\times$. The Dakota
Lithium 20~Ah pack remains the specification reference
\cite{dakota2023battery}, but importing it lands near \num{9900}~EGP\indflag{}
(catalogue $\approx$150~USD with the \num{1.38} factor), roughly twice the
local equivalent, so it was rejected on landed cost.

\subsection{Reconciliation with the design report's 5760 EGP}
The design report's prototype table closes at about \num{5760}~EGP. That
figure and this one describe the same robot at different levels of honesty
about parts, and the gap of roughly \num{22700}~EGP decomposes cleanly:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Encoder drivetrain, $+$\num{11600}.} The report carried two
        generic gearmotors at \num{220}~EGP with no encoder and no driver
        boards. The ROS~2 architecture, frozen later, requires wheel
        encoders for odometry; no local motor in stock meets both the
        \SI{1.03}{\newton\metre} line and the encoder line, so the
        drivetrain becomes a Pololu import at the \num{1.38} landed factor.
  \item \textbf{Raspberry Pi tier, $+$\num{5100}.} The report priced a
        single MCU at \num{350}~EGP. The two-tier compute design adds the
        Pi 4B.
  \item \textbf{Battery class, $+$\num{3600}.} The report carried a
        7--12~\si{\ampere\hour} pack at \num{900}~EGP; the frozen
        requirement is a \SI{20}{\ampere\hour} LFP pack with a
        \SI{20}{\ampere} BMS.
  \item \textbf{Bench supply, $+$\num{1250}.} The report had no mains
        supply; the five-day plan's indoor condensation test needs one, and
        driving all four tiles needs \SI{29}{\ampere}, not \SI{10}{\ampere}.
  \item \textbf{Real thermal and brush hardware, $+$\num{1900} net.} CPU
        coolers at market price replace the \num{150}~EGP heat-sink
        estimate, and a real geared brush motor replaces the
        \num{200}~EGP brush-and-spray line. The report's \SI{50}{\Wp} PV
        module ($-$\num{700}) is dropped: the bench charges from mains.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
The one number that moved the other way is the tile itself: \num{120}~EGP
live against the \num{130}~EGP design basis, so the headline TEC costing
assumption held. If the encoder requirement were waived for the demo and the
GA25-370 fallback used with ESP32-only control, the bench would land near
\num{10000}~EGP; the figure above is the price of demonstrating the actual
thesis architecture rather than a simplified stand-in.

\section{Conclusion}
% =============================================================================

Every frozen requirement now has a named market part with a stated margin.
The bench demonstrator is sourceable for \num{28475}~EGP, with all
schedule-critical lines in Cairo stock confirmed live on 12 June 2026 and
only the three Pololu motors shipping; the gap to the design report's
\num{5760}~EGP estimate is the documented price of encoders, the Pi tier,
the bench supply, and the larger battery, not price drift. The full robot
prices at \num{218360}~EGP ($\approx$\num{4549}~USD), dominated by the
station battery bank at \SI{58.6}{\percent} and the MPPT pair at
\SI{8.7}{\percent}. The TEC tile, the assumption the whole costing rests on,
checked in below its design price. The one correction this exercise forced
on the record: the transcript's Pololu part numbers no longer mean what they
meant when shortlisted, and the BOM now names the current catalogue items
\#4756 and \#4743 with the datasheet values re-verified.
