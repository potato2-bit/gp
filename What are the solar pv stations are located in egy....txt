Egypt has successfully positioned itself as a regional hub for renewable energy, with solar power playing a central role.1 The country's solar infrastructure is dominated by Industrial (Utility-Scale) projects, though there are several Medium and Small off-grid stations serving remote areas.


The following is a detailed breakdown of the solar PV stations in Egypt, categorized by size.
1. Industrial / Utility-Scale Stations
These are massive projects connected to the national high-voltage grid, designed to power hundreds of thousands of homes.
Benban Solar Park
* Size: Massive Industrial (One of the largest in the world)2

* Capacity: ~1,650 MW (1.65 GW) total capacity.
* Location: Benban, Aswan Governorate (Western Desert).3

* Details: This is not just one station but a cluster of 41 separate solar power plants (mostly ~50 MW each) developed by different companies (e.g., Scatec, Infinity, ACWA Power, Alcazar Energy) in a single park.
* Status: Fully Operational.
* Data & Resources:
   * World Bank / IFC Project Data: Nubian Suns Solar Park
   * NREA (New & Renewable Energy Authority): Benban Project Overview
   * Developer Example (Scatec): Scatec Egypt Projects
Kom Ombo Solar Plant
   * Size: Industrial
   * Capacity: 200 MW.4

   * Location: Kom Ombo, Aswan (approx.5 20km from Benban).

   * Details: A single-site utility-scale plant developed by ACWA Power.6 It uses bifacial solar panels (capturing sunlight from both sides) to increase efficiency.7

   * Status: Operational (Inaugurated recently).
   * Data & Resources:
      * ACWA Power Project Page: Kom Ombo PV
      * EBRD Project Summary: Kom Ombo Solar Details
Zaafarana Solar Plant
      * Size: Industrial
      * Capacity: 50 MW.8

      * Location: Zaafarana, Red Sea Governorate.9

      * Details: While Zaafarana is famous for wind farms, this PV plant was built to diversify the site's output. It was developed with German financing (KfW).
      * Status: Operational.
      * Data & Resources:
         * Belectric (EPC Contractor): Zaafarana Project News (Search "Egypt" in their press releases for technical specifics).
Kuraymat Integrated Solar Combined Cycle (ISCC)
         * Size: Industrial (Hybrid)
         * Capacity: ~140 MW Total (Solar component contributes ~20 MW thermal equivalent).
         * Location: Atfih, Giza (South of Cairo).
         * Details: This is a hybrid plant using natural gas and a solar field of parabolic troughs (CSP technology, not PV).10 It was Egypt's first major foray into solar thermal power.

         * Status: Operational since 2011.
         * Data & Resources:
            * NREA Project Page: Kuraymat ISCC Details

2. Medium-Scale Stations
These projects typically power specific cities, tourism districts, or industrial zones.
Hurghada Solar PV Plant
            * Size: Medium
            * Capacity: 20 MW (+ Battery Storage).11

            * Location: Hurghada, Red Sea.12

            * Details: Developed in cooperation with JICA (Japan), this plant is unique because it includes battery storage to stabilize the grid for the tourism-heavy city.13

            * Status: Under Development/Construction.
            * Data & Resources:
               * JICA Project Brief: Hurghada PV Project
Siwa Solar Plant
               * Size: Medium
               * Capacity: 10 MW.
               * Location: Siwa Oasis, Western Desert.
               * Details: An off-grid/hybrid solution designed to reduce the oasis's reliance on diesel generators.
               * Status: Operational.
               * Data & Resources:
               * Masdar (Developer): Siwa Solar PV Plant
